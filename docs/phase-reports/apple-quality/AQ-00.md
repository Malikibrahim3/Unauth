## Baseline visual diagnosis

The captures confirm the implementation brief:

1. Overview is a stack of similarly bordered regions with no dominant object.
2. Overview’s chart region can occupy most of the viewport as unresolved blank
   space while the surrounding frame still looks “complete”.
3. Equal KPI cells compete with the analytical question.
4. Work begins with two large metrics and a risk panel before the actual queue.
5. Cases combines a KPI slab, dense toolbar, master list, and preview with
   several equal visual boundaries.
6. Case detail is visually fragmented and captured in an unresolved
   evidence-loading state.
7. The shell is assembled from workspace, source, search, notification, and
   navigation widgets rather than three composed planes.
8. Normal operating copy and metadata become texture when the screenshot is
   reduced.
