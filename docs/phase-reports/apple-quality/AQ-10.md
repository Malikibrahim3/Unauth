## Cutover

The authenticated product now uses one Apple-disciplined, browser-native visual
system. The work deliberately adopts Apple's hierarchy, restraint, legibility,
feedback, and progressive-disclosure principles without reproducing macOS or
iOS chrome.

It does not visually restyle public marketing, legal, authentication,
onboarding, the helpdesk widget, or the browser extension.
