### Prospective human test protocol

Recruitment/contact is not authorised by this plan. When separately authorised, test **10 target merchants** spanning owner/support/operations/finance and **10 adults unfamiliar with ecommerce software**, with a mix of desktop and phone presentations. This is a small directional acceptance sample, not market-wide statistical proof. Record device, experience, verbatim answers with consent, assistance given, tasks completed and critical misunderstandings. Do not show internal product documents or teach the terms first.

1. Show hero for five seconds, then hide: “What does this product help with?” Credit both customer-order problems and eligible merchant money-back work, not exact wording.
2. Allow a thirty-second skim: “What would Unauth do, and what would your team still do?” Credit current versus intended supported execution and human approval; reject universal automation.
3. Present each case without opening details: explain the decisive evidence, rule and next action. Damage must distinguish £96 refund from £33 replacement cost; duplicate must identify previous same-item compensation without alleging fraud.
4. Recovery: “What needs doing next? Has this claim been submitted? Is the money guaranteed?” Reader must identify current blocker and later illustrative path accurately.
5. Money: “Does £150 mean available, approved or received here? What does £98 mean?” Require the correct displayed stage and unrecovered refund balance, not total loss/profit.
6. Adoption: “Where does this fit with your existing tools? What work would it remove or connect? What happens if you click the main button?” Credit a specific process improvement and fictional desktop walkthrough, not a live action or free trial.

Score a predetermined answer key with ten binary checkpoints across these tasks, including both jobs, current role split, target scope, actual gate reasoning, remedy comparison, earlier-compensation meaning, next recovery action, financial stages, remaining balance and next-step expectation. Correct misunderstandings after recording the unassisted result; correction cannot turn that result into a pass. Report raw counts, not only percentages.
