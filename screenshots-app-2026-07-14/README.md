# Unauth app screenshot capture

Captured 2026-07-14 from the local app at `http://localhost:3000`.

The PNGs are full-page screenshots. The `public_landing_preview_*.png` and
`public_landing_faq_expanded.png` files are interactive-state captures.

Coverage includes:

- Public landing, demo, pricing, automation hero, auth, and legal pages
- Primary authenticated product surfaces and navigation areas
- Settings, integration, help, reporting, import, and workflow pages
- Landing-page preview and FAQ expanded states

Some data-dependent detail/settings routes returned unstable local-app
responses during capture and are not represented by a successful PNG:
`/customers/[id]`, `/settings/team`, `/settings/audit-trail`,
`/settings/api-integrations`, `/settings/integrations/gorgias`,
`/reports/records`, and `/help/integrations/yuma`.
